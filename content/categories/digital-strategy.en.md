## Who this is for

This category is for business owners who have to decide where the digital budget goes: a website, an app, an internal system, or fixing what is already there. The focus is the decision, not the technology.

What we cover:

- **Digital transformation for SMEs**, step by step and sized to the business, not one big project all at once.
- **How to measure the return on digital spend**, including costs that are easy to miss, such as maintenance and the team's time.
- **The hidden cost of the wrong software**: extra manual work, scattered data, and expensive migrations.
- **Digital presence**: what prospective customers actually see before they contact you.

If you are drawing up a plan, start with the SME digital transformation guide and the guide to measuring returns.
