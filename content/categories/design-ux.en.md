## Who this is for

This category is for businesses that have an app or website whose users get confused, keep asking support for help, or drop off before they buy or sign up.

Design here is not about colours and taste. We write about how screens and flows move business numbers:

- **What UX design is worth in money**: fewer support questions, more transactions completed.
- **Accessibility**: interfaces everyone can use, including people with low vision or a budget phone.
- **Design that can be built straight away**: working screens, not pictures in a slide file.

Start with the guide on the business value of UX design, then go on to the article closest to the problem in your product.
