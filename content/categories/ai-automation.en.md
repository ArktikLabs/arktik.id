## Who this is for

This category is for businesses whose teams still spend working hours on repetitive tasks: copying data between systems, sorting incoming requests, reading and summarising documents, or answering the same question again and again.

We write about AI and automation from the business side, not the technology side:

- **Which processes are worth automating first**, and which are better left manual.
- **Real use cases** for small and mid-sized businesses, not demos that only look good in a slide deck.
- **How to work out whether it pays**: the cost to build and run it, against the working hours it saves.
- **The risks to manage**, such as customer data and AI answers that are wrong.

If you are just starting, read the strategy guide first, then pick the example closest to a process in your business.
