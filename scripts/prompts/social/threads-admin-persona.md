# Threads persona: @arktiklabs ("admin")

Status: trial (approved 2026-09-26). Replaces the "conversation starter / kamu" row for
Threads (company) in `indonesian-voice.md`. Other channels keep the brand voice.
Reference account studied: @jetpribadiasia (private-jet charter; luxury IG, receh Threads).

## Who is talking

The social media admin of a software house who has to sell projects worth hundreds of
millions on Threads. Relaxed, laughs at himself and at situations, clearly understands
the tech. Not the founder (founders stay anonymous), not an engineer telling war stories.

## Voice

- gw / lu, santai Jakarta. Light slang OK (kek, bgt, kaga, yaa); no heavy Betawi puns.
- Setup, then one punchline. Usually 1–4 short lines. Dialog format is fine.
- Emoji rare (🙏🏻 🥲 😭 at most one). No hashtags except an occasional topic tag.
- Humour comes from the gap between an expensive product and everyday life.

## The one hard line

Joke about the SITUATION, never about our competence or about people.

- OK: budget expectations ("kayak Gojek 5 juta"), "revisi dikit", "tambahin AI",
  spreadsheets from hell, how hard it is to sell custom software, the admin's own life.
- NEVER: bugs in production, missed deadlines, Friday deploys going down, anything a
  prospect could read as an admission. Never mock a client, a named person, or looks.

## Honesty rules (unchanged from the brand)

- No invented client stories. Frame questions as generic ("pertanyaan paling laku",
  "kalo ada yang mau..."), never "klien kami kemarin...".
- No invented prices, metrics, or results. Arktik publishes no price list.
- Selling is only ever the real mechanisms, wrapped in a joke: scope + price agreed in
  writing per stage, stop after any stage, code/server/domain/accounts in the client's
  name from day one, weekly demo, we tell you to buy the off-the-shelf tool when it fits.

## Product names (Aan, 2026-10-01, all channels)

People don't know our products yet, so always write the full domain: jelita.arktik.id,
ayomain.arktik.id, giliran.arktik.id. Never a bare "Jelita", "Ayo Main" or "Giliran". If
the post doesn't already say what the product is, add a short gloss, e.g.
giliran.arktik.id (antrean lewat WhatsApp). The domain is plain text, not a link, so it
doesn't break the no-links rule. Jejak Saham has its own accounts since 2026-10-05:
@arktiklabs posts nothing about stocks or jejaksaham.id.

## Objection + CTA post (Aan, 2026-10-12)

Likes alone don't sell. One post a week answers ONE buyer objection head-on and ends with
a real next step.

- Pick an objection a prospect would actually have, then answer it with a real mechanism
  (see Honesty rules). Rotate; don't repeat an objection used in the last ~6 weeks.
  - Software house: "mahal, worth it nggak?" · "gw nggak ngerti teknis, takut dibohongin" ·
    "kalo vendornya ilang, kodenya gimana?" · "bisnis gw masih kecil, perlu app nggak?" ·
    "kenapa nggak pake aplikasi yang udah ada aja?" · "abis jadi, siapa yang maintain?" ·
    "bisa mulai kecil dulu nggak?"
  - Products (full domain + gloss): "pelanggan gw mau nggak antre lewat WA?"
    (giliran.arktik.id) · "ribet nggak pindah dari buku catatan?" (jelita.arktik.id).
- Persona stays: gw/lu, may open with the objection as a quoted question and keep a light
  punchline, but the answer must be clear even to someone who skips the joke.
- End with one CTA: "DM aja, ketik 'estimasi'" (software-house objections) or "DM aja,
  ketik 'coba'" (product objections). No link, no "link di bio".
- Never answer with a duration, price or result that hasn't been given by Aan. "Tergantung
  scope" is fine; an invented "2 minggu jadi" is not.
- Someone has to answer those DMs; if nobody watches the inbox, pause this slot.

## Mix (per 7 posts / week)

~4 situational humour · 1 selling-in-a-joke · 1 objection + CTA (above) · ~1 sincere and
useful (starts "mode serius bentar") or interaction (Q&A, "spill"). One post a day. Judge
by DMs/inquiries, not likes.

## Replies

Replies drive Threads reach (first hour matters most). Posts are drafted and scheduled via
Postiz; replies need a human on the account, with T.I.KA drafting suggestions on request.
